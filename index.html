Process

How we work

Every project follows the same six steps, so you know what to expect from consultation to final walkthrough.

01

Consultation

We discuss the project, design, tile, and your expectations.

02

Measurements

We measure the space and determine the amount of tile and material needed.

03

Preparation

We prepare the surface properly before any tile goes down.

04

Installation

Tile goes in with attention to layout, spacing, level, and detail.

05

Finishing

Grout, caulking, trim, and final details are completed.

06

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished project with you to make sure you're satisfied.
